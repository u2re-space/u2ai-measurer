# Brand colours

The brand colours of the project, in the notation each consumer needs.

| Name | Hex | OKLCH | Use |
|------|-----|-------|-----|
| Ink | `#1c1c1b` | `oklch(0.23 0.002 108)` | The text and the dark surfaces. |
| Paper | `#fdfdfd` | `oklch(0.9944 0.0012 271.17)` | The light surfaces. |
| Signal | `#f1463f` | `oklch(0.6414 0.2087 27.18)` | The call to action. |

The contrast of Ink on Paper is 15.8:1 and the contrast of Signal on Paper is 3.7:1: the
signal colour is for large text and for the edges of the controls, never for body copy.
